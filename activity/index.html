About ACSS

Our Mission & Core Values

Driven by curiosity, fueled by code, and dedicated to academic and technical excellence.

Our Mission

To cultivate a thriving environment for Computer Science students through high-quality technical workshops, peer mentorship, hackathons, and real-world software development initiatives.

Our Vision

To become a premier student organization recognized for producing visionary software engineers, innovators, and technological leaders who solve global challenges through code.
